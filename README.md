# Methodology for Shariah-Compliant Investment Screening

An independent research project that screens public companies against a strict, zero-tolerance Shariah standard. It looks at the actual financial structures and contracts behind a company's financing and cash holdings instead of accepting "Islamic" labels at face value.

**[Read the paper (PDF)](methodology.pdf)**

## What it covers

- Screening criteria for core business, conventional debt, interest income, and treasury practices
- Rules for evaluating Murabaha, tawarruq, Ijara, and Sukuk contracts
- A screening process and example findings for 12 companies and funds

## Disclaimer

This is non-expert, independent research. It is not a religious ruling (fatwa) and is not investment, financial, religious, or legal advice. Verify any conclusion with a qualified scholar before acting on it.
